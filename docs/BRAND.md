# BRAC Design System – Microfinance (Agriculture Insurance)

This is the complete design reference for BRAC Microfinance materials: Bengali crop 1-pagers, slide decks (such as the BKB partnership deck), reports and other print or digital items. It combines the **BRAC Brand Book**, the **official BRAC PowerPoint template**, the **official logo pack and brand motif**, and the working standards we have used and approved.

Each rule is labelled with its source:

- **[Brand book]**: an official BRAC rule. Do not change it.
- **[Template]**: built into the official BRAC PowerPoint template.
- **[Project standard]**: our own approved working rule, built on the brand book.

If a project standard ever conflicts with the brand book, the brand book wins.

### 2.5 Placement [Brand book]

Place the logo off-centre, **top-left or bottom-right**.

### 2.6 Never [Brand book]

- Separate the symbol from the wordmark
- Alter or ungroup the logo in any way
- Scale it unevenly, stretch or distort it
- Rotate or tilt it
- Substitute its colour or use several colours within it
- Put a country or programme name next to it (for example "BRAC UGANDA" or "BRAC EDUCATION")

The programme name, **মাইক্রোফাইন্যান্স কর্মসূচি**, goes in the footer bar, never beside the logo.

## 5. Brand motif: "Always a way" stitch [Brand book]

**Story.** The motif is a running stitch from *nakshi katha*, a centuries-old Bengali embroidery tradition that women use to record the stories of their lives. It started from one of the fluid shapes of the BRAC logo. That shape became a thread, and the thread became the stitch. Its path is not simple or straight; it flows unpredictably, like the rivers of Bangladesh, and like the journey of unmaking inequality.

**File.** The motif is in `Brand motif.indd` (InDesign). Export it as SVG or PNG before using it in slides or 1-pagers. Never redraw it.

**Rules:**

- The stitch has three points and is uneven.
- It must **never dominate** an image, a page design or a document.
- It must have **no more than two peaks** and **never form a loop**.
- **Never increase the stroke weight** and never edit the stitch file.
- Its colour should complement the colours in the image on the same spread.
- Use it only on selected pages, as a supporting device.
- Lay it **behind the subject of the photo** where possible, or overlay it subtly where it doesn't distract from the focal point.

## 6. Layout and design style [Brand book]

- **White space is as important as images and copy.** Balance white space, copy and imagery so they complement each other.
- With large amounts of copy, **leave images out** so the page doesn't feel heavy.
- Designs should be integrated, with nothing blocking the images. Give images room to stand out, with space for the message.
- Keep the style dynamic and the themes consistent throughout.

[Project standard]

- Margins: at least 0.5 in (slides), 0.6 in for content, 50 px (A4 1-pager).
- One message per slide or per card.
- Every slide or section has a visual element: an icon, chart, flow, stat or photo.
- No decorative accent lines under titles and no edge stripes on slides. Use tinted cards, icons and white space instead.
- Left-align body text. Centre text only inside flow boxes and chips.
- Use simple **line icons** (rounded caps, about a 2–3 px stroke) in brand colours, inside a light-tint or white circle. No clip-art and no emojis.

## 7. Photography [Brand book]

| Principle | Rule |
|---|---|
| Concept | Show "always a way" through **movement**. Subjects should be active and in motion, never staged or static. |
| Framing | The background sets the scene and can show the subject's journey. Use angles that create dynamic lines. |
| Crop | Crop afterwards to focus on important moments, emotions and actions. |
| Perspective | Vary the angles. **Avoid perspectives that put the viewer in a position of power**, such as shooting from above. Use eye level. |
| Emotion | Show real emotion, from thoughtful stares to happiness, including success, tenacity and overcoming a challenge. |
| Feel | Bright, natural, honest. |
| Technical | At least **4000 × 3000 px**, JPEG or RAW, with metadata embedded. |
| Consent | Obtain **signed consent** before publishing anyone identifiable. Use **aliases** for people in vulnerable situations; this is mandatory. |

[Project standard] On 1-pagers, give photos rounded corners of 16–18 px and a soft shadow.

## 10. Writing style [Brand book]

### 10.1 Principles

- Portray **people as their own heroes**. Don't portray BRAC as the saviour.
- Use **"people", "participants" or "clients"**, never "beneficiaries".
- Put the person first, then the situation: "people living in poverty", not "the poor".
- Use **"survivor"**, never "victim".
- Use gender-neutral language: chairperson, police officer, staff, humankind, human-made.
- Use inclusive terms: "people living with disabilities", "lower-income countries", "people living in rural areas".
- BRAC doesn't "empower" people. It provides tools such as education, microfinance and health services. Empowerment may be an eventual impact.
- Distinguish **activities → outputs → outcomes → impacts**.
- Use **British English**: colour, organisation, centre, programme.
- Use simple, short sentences. Avoid informal words unless quoting someone directly.
- Italicise non-English words and explain them in brackets the first time, for example *haor* (wetland).

### 10.2 Bengali materials [Project standard]

1. Keep text short. Use one line per point, and use flows rather than paragraphs.
2. Spelling follows **Prothom Alo**. When proofreading, fix only spelling; don't change wording or style.
3. Confirmed word choices:

   | Use | Not |
   |---|---|
   | শাকসবজি | শাক-সবজি |
   | ঔষধ | ওষুধ |
   | কীটনাশক | বালাইনাশক (farmers know কীটনাশক better) |
   | ক্যান্সার | — |
   | সাথে | সঙ্গে |
   | ঠান্ডা | — |
   | খাদ্য আঁশ | — |
   | ভিটামিন সি | — |
   | অংশগ্রহণকারী / কৃষক | উপকারভোগী |

4. Use কী for "what" (লাভ কী?) and কি for yes/no questions (কি না).
5. Numbers: Bengali numerals, a comma for thousands (৳৬,০০০), and an en dash for ranges.
6. No emojis in print materials.
7. Explain technical terms in brackets the first time they appear, for example সমন্বিত বালাই ব্যবস্থাপনা (IPM).
8. Check that figures are consistent across the page, for example ৪০০ বর্গফুট = ০.৯২ শতাংশ.

### 10.3 Executive and partner decks in English [Project standard]

- Use the partner's language. For banks: overdue, classified loans and NPL, provisioning, recovery, write-off, risk-adjusted profitability.
- Label every illustrative figure **"Illustrative"** on the slide itself.
- Put a source line on every slide with data (10 pt, grey, bottom-left, clear of the logo).
- Flag public figures as "to be verified against [partner] MIS and audited statements" until they are confirmed.
- Slide titles are full-sentence messages, with no full stop at the end.

## 11. Component library [Project standard]

| Component | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Header (1-pager)** | Logo top-left. Title right-aligned in magenta, subtitle in grey below it. A thin pink divider line with a magenta dot. Soft pink gradient background, with a faint leaf motif top-right. |
| **Section kicker (slides)** | 12 pt bold UPPER CASE magenta label above the title, for example "WHY NOW" or "BUSINESS CASE". |
| **Section card (1-pager)** | White card with an 18 px radius, a 2 px theme border and a 9 px theme-colour left edge. Numbered badge (42 px circle, theme colour, white Bengali numeral, white ring). Line icon in a light-fill circle (96–100 px). Icon column 150 px wide. |
| **Tinted card (slides)** | Rounded rectangle (radius about 0.1 in) in a neutral, teal or magenta tint. No edge stripes. Icon in a white circle, bold heading, grey description. |
| **Numbered list** | Bengali numerals ১. ২. ৩. with hanging indent. For two columns, use two separate lists side by side. |
| **Sub-heading** | Amber dot (`#FFA100`) followed by bold dark text, for example "• মনে রাখবেন" or "• কীভাবে করবেন?". |
| **Process flow (chain)** | 4–6 rounded boxes of 1–4 words each, in the theme's light fill, joined by **drawn** orange chevrons or arrows (never the "→" text character). The final box is magenta or pink. |
| **Two-lane flow** | Top lane "Without …" in neutral boxes, ending in a brick-red box. Bottom lane "With …" in teal tint, ending in a magenta box. |
| **Step flow (numbered)** | Taller boxes, each with a numbered circle on its top edge, a bold step title and a short instruction. White arrows in orange circles. The final result step is pink. |
| **Stat boxes** | 3–5 equal boxes, each with a large magenta number and a short label underneath. |
| **Formula chips** | Teal-tint chips joined by + signs, with the cost chip in a red tint after a − sign. Used for a "net benefit" equation. |
| **Tags (pills)** | Cream fill with an amber border. For material lists and pest names. |
| **Tiles** | A 2 × 2, 2 × 3 or 2 × 4 grid of boxes, each with a bold title and a one-line description. |
| **Quote box** | Cream fill with a large amber quote mark. One key message per section. |
| **"ILLUSTRATIVE" badge** | Orange pill with near-black bold 11 pt text. |
| **Statement strip** | Full-width magenta-tint bar with one bold sentence. One per slide at most. |
| **Statement slide** | Solid magenta background with the white logo (template layout), white kicker and white bold statement. Used for the proposition and the close. |
| **Charts** | Native, editable charts. Neutral grey for the baseline and magenta for the highlight (teal and brick red when a story needs them). Data labels 14 pt bold, no gridlines, no legend for a single series, title 14 pt bold. |
| **Timeline / Gantt** | Label column on the left (bold phase name, grey description), with month ticks and rounded bars in teal → magenta → near-black → orange. |
| **Slogan box (1-pager)** | Pink box with a lightbulb icon on the left (white on a magenta circle), the slogan centred in bold magenta, and a "people" line icon on the right. **One slogan per page only.** |
| **Footer (1-pager)** | Magenta bar, 52 px, containing only **মাইক্রোফাইন্যান্স কর্মসূচি**, left-aligned in white. No tagline and no web address. |

Slide rules [Project standard]:

- Content area: from y 0.4 in to y 6.5 in. Keep the **bottom-right corner clear** for the logo (x > 11.7 in, y > 6.6 in).

- Source line: 10 pt grey, bottom-left, at y about 6.85 in.
- At most 6 bullets or items per block, and body text at least 14 pt.
- **Footer bar:** the official template uses only the logo. Add the magenta মাইক্রোফাইন্যান্স কর্মসূচি bar only on internal or field Bengali decks if that's requested; it's off by default.
- Put speaker notes on every slide of an executive deck.
- Executive deck structure (BKB model): Cover → Proposition (magenta) → Executive summary → Problem (exposure, why now, leakage) → Solution (chain, mechanism, architecture, model) → Proof (scope, business case, pilot and KPIs) → Risks → Roadmap → Decision requested → Closing statement (magenta) → Appendix of sources → End slide.

## 14. Pre-publish checklist

**Brand**

- [ ] Official logo file used (magenta by default; white on colour or dark photos), not stretched, rotated or recoloured
- [ ] Logo top-left or bottom-right, with the exclusion zone kept clear
- [ ] No programme or country name beside the logo
